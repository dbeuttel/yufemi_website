FAQ

Questions owners ask us.

What size of business do you work with?

Mostly owner-led businesses and nonprofits with roughly 5 to 100 people. Big enough to have real decisions, small enough that the owner or director is still making them.

How is this different from free help like SCORE or the SBTDC?

Those programs are excellent and we recommend them. They advise; we do the work: gathering and analyzing your data, researching your market, and building a plan with your team. Many clients use both.

How much does a project cost?

Every engagement has a fixed scope and price agreed before we start. The Clarity Sprint starts from $2,500; larger planning and research projects are quoted after a free discovery call.

Do you only work in Raleigh?

We're based in Raleigh and work in person across the Triangle, including Durham, Cary and Chapel Hill. We also work remotely with organizations across North Carolina.

What do I actually get at the end?

A short, plain-language decision document: what the evidence says, what we recommend, and a 90-day action plan with owners and dates. No 80-page binder.

Got a decision on your desk?

Tell us about it. In a free 30-minute call we'll help you frame the question, and tell you honestly whether we're the right people to answer it.
