New to sea moss?

It's a seaweed, not a moss.

Sea moss is a red seaweed that grows on rocky ocean shores. People have eaten it for centuries, from Irish coastal kitchens to Caribbean "Irish moss" drinks, for its minerals, iodine and naturally silky texture.

Read Sea Moss 101 →
  • 🌊Grows in the oceanOn rocks in the cold North Atlantic and warm Caribbean waters.
  • 🤲Harvested by handPicked from the wild or grown on ocean ropes, then sun-dried.
  • ✨Naturally rich in mineralsIodine, calcium, magnesium, iron, potassium and gut-friendly fiber.
  • 🥄A little goes a long wayIt's high in iodine, so a spoonful a day is plenty.

FAQ

Good questions.

How do I use sea moss gel?

Stir a spoonful into a smoothie, juice, tea, oatmeal or yogurt, or take it straight from the spoon. Start small and see how your body feels.

How long does sea moss gel last?

Kept in an airtight jar in the refrigerator, sea moss gel typically stays fresh for 3 to 4 weeks. You can also freeze it in ice cube trays for up to 6 months.

Are there any side effects?

Sea moss is naturally high in iodine, so anyone with a thyroid condition should talk to a healthcare provider before using it. As with any new food or supplement, start with a small amount.

Is sea moss safe during pregnancy?

Please check with your OB-GYN or primary care provider before adding any new supplement while pregnant or nursing.
